Custom Skies Beta

Replace the sky Roblox uses by default. Your choice is applied to Keystrap's copy of Roblox on every launch. Games that set their own sky keep theirs.

To import a sky, choose its six images (PNG, JPG or BMP) or six .tex/.dds textures at once. Name them so the side is at the end: _bk, _dn, _ft, _lf, _rt and _up, or _back, _down, _front, _left, _right and _up.

Crosshairs Beta

Replace the cursor Roblox shows over the game and the crosshair in Shift Lock and first person. Your choice is applied to Keystrap's copy of Roblox on every launch. Games that set their own cursor keep theirs.

Launching

Open Roblox links with Keystrap
Play buttons on roblox.com start Roblox through Keystrap, with your active profile's flags.
Close Keystrap after Roblox starts
With multi-instance on, Keystrap minimizes instead so extra windows keep working. Playtime is only tracked while Keystrap is open.
Cap Menu at 60 FPS (Reduce Fan Noise)
When Keystrap opens the Roblox home screen, Roblox starts at 60 FPS to keep your GPU quiet. Game links from roblox.com still start with your Unlock FPS choice. Roblox only reads this when it starts, so a game you join from the home screen stays at 60 FPS until you launch again.

Terms of Service

Last updated 24 October 2026

1. What Keystrap is

Keystrap is an independent, open-source launcher for Roblox. It is not made by, affiliated with, or endorsed by Roblox Corporation.

2. Downloading Roblox

Keystrap downloads the Roblox client directly from Roblox's own servers, checks each file against Roblox's published checksums, and keeps it in its own folder.

3. Your account

Keystrap never asks for your Roblox password and never reads your login cookie. You remain bound by the Roblox Terms of Use while playing.

4. Flags

FastFlags are applied at your own risk. Roblox may change, ignore or remove any flag without notice, and some games may behave differently.

5. Data

Profiles, settings and logs are stored only on your computer. Keystrap sends the developers' Discord only these:

A suggestion, when you submit one from the Support page: its text, your Windows user name and the Keystrap version.

A crash report, if Keystrap crashes: the error and its technical details, the Keystrap version, your active profile's name, your Windows version and the time. Your Windows user name and folder paths are removed first. You can turn crash reports off in Settings.

6. No warranty

Keystrap is provided as is, without warranty of any kind.
